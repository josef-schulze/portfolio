# About Josef Schulze

I am a Senior Data Engineer with over 20 years of experience in IT, database development, and building enterprise data solutions. My career includes 14 years at SBC/AT&T and 7+ years at Bayer/Monsanto, where I specialized in designing and maintaining complex ETL pipelines, database architecture, and data migrations.

## Career Highlights

- Led key components of the **Conductor Pipeline**, a custom ETL platform that enabled the successful consolidation of 13 regional Salesforce instances into a single global organization.
- Designed and optimized PostgreSQL databases, Python orchestration scripts, and automation processes that supported large-scale data movements while maintaining compliance and data integrity.
- Built reusable toolkits and complex SQL solutions that improved efficiency across multiple teams.
- Mentored developers and collaborated closely with stakeholders in agile environments.
